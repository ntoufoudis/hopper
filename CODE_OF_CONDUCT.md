# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We - the contributors and maintainers of Hopper - want participation in this project to be a
welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act and interact
in ways that keep the community open, friendly, and healthy.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community thrive includes:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and ways of working.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people in it, not just for ourselves.
- Showing empathy and patience toward other community members.

Behaviour that is unacceptable includes:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address) without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces - the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other official channel - and also applies when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it privately to the
maintainers at [info@ntoufoudis.com](mailto:info@ntoufoudis.com), or through GitHub's private
reporting on the repository. Reports will be handled confidentially, and we will respond as
promptly as is practical.

When reporting, please include what happened, where and when, who was involved, and any
context (links, screenshots) that would help us understand the situation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. In response to
behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful, they may take any action they
consider appropriate, proportionate to the severity of the violation - including a private
warning, a temporary restriction from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who do
not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent
consequences as determined by other maintainers.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is informed by widely-used open-source community standards, including the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org). It is written for this project
and may be adapted as the community grows.
